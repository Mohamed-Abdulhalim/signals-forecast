What is being tested

The {{ coverage_pct }}% range

Each forecast gives a low and a high price for {{ horizon_days }} days later. If the method is honest, the actual price should land inside about {{ coverage_pct }}% of the time. Much lower means the ranges are too narrow; much higher means they are too wide to be useful.

The trend signal

"Uptrend" means the price is higher than {{ trend_days }} trading days ago. The test is whether the price is higher again {{ horizon_days }} days later. A coin flip scores 50%. Always saying "up" scores whatever share of months happened to rise, so that is the bar to beat.
