What is being tested

The {{ coverage_pct }}% range

Each forecast gives a low and a high price for {{ horizon_days }} days later. If the method is honest, the actual price should land inside about {{ coverage_pct }}% of the time. Much lower means the ranges are too narrow; much higher means they are too wide to be useful.

The trend signal

"Uptrend" means the price is higher than {{ trend_days }} trading days ago. The test is whether the price is higher again {{ horizon_days }} days later. A coin flip scores 50%. Always saying "up" scores whatever share of months happened to rise, so that is the bar to beat.

How much to trust these numbers

  • Forecasts overlap. Each forecast looks {{ horizon_days }} days ahead and a new one is made every week in the backtest and every weekday live, so neighbouring forecasts share most of their outcome. The scored counts are much larger than the number of independent tests. As a rough guide, a trend result within about 10 points of 50% cannot be told apart from luck, and a range result within about 6 points of the target is consistent with a well calibrated method.
  • The backtest is not a blind test. The model was built and adjusted while this history was available, so the backtest cannot be treated as out-of-sample. Only live forecasts, made before the outcome was known, are a real test.{% if track.live.first_due %} The first of them are scored from {{ track.live.first_due }}.{% endif %}
  • Live forecasts are write-once. Once a live forecast is stored, a database rule rejects any attempt to change or delete it, and the daily job only adds new prices without rewriting old ones.
