How it works

Automated daily · no manual edits
01

Prices collected every weekday

One source per market, one unit per market. Obvious one-day data glitches are filtered out before anything is stored.

02

A range, not a price target

Point forecasts for commodities rarely beat "tomorrow looks like today". EdgePulse instead estimates how far each market could move in {{ horizon_days }} days, using its own history and current volatility.

03

Scored in public

Every forecast is stored before the outcome is known and checked automatically when it comes due. The track record compares it with a coin flip and with "always up".
