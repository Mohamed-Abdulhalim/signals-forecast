Each row shows how far the price could move in either direction, on one shared scale, so markets can be compared at a glance. Grain futures are converted from cents to dollars. Select a row to see its chart.

How it works

Prices come in every weekday

One source and one unit per market. Obvious one-day data glitches are filtered out before anything is stored.

A range, not a price target

Point forecasts for commodities rarely beat "tomorrow looks like today". EdgePulse estimates how far each market could move in {{ horizon_days }} days, using its own history and current volatility.

Every forecast is scored

Each forecast is stored before the outcome is known and checked automatically when it comes due, against a coin flip and against always saying up.
